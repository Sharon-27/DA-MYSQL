Topic:- Date and Time functions

1.CURDATE()--->CURRENT DATE
2.CURTIME()--->CURRENT TIME
3.NOW(); CURRENT DATE AND TIME
4.DATE(); EXTRACTED DATE PART
5.TIME(); EXTRACTED TIME PART
6. YEAR(); EXTRACT YEAR
7.MONTH(); EXTRACT MONTH
8.DAY()/ DAYOFMONTH(): EXTRACT DAY OF THE MONTH
9.WEEK(): EXTRACT WEEK NUMBER
10.DAYOFWEEK(): EXTRACT DAY OF THE WEEK
11.DATE_ADD(): ADD TIME TO A DATE
12.DATE_SUB(): SUBTRACT TIME FROM A DATE
13. DATEDIFF(): DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO DATES
14.TIMESTAMPDIFF(): DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO DATETIME VALUES.





Type 'help;' or '\h' for help. Type '\c' to clear the current input statement.
