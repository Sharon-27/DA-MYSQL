---> clauses:-
----Definition:- In MYSQL clauses are used to control how data is filtered, grouped, sorted or modified in SQL Queries.
they make SQL statements more meaningful and powerful.


Where clause:-

filters records based on specified conditions where clause can be used as comparision operators, logical operators and... etc operators.


---> Syntax:-
select column1,column2,column3.... from table where condition;

Group by clause
---
The group by clause is used to group rows having the same values in one or more columns, it is commonly used with aggregate functions such as min(),max(),sum(),count(),avg()

---> Syntax:-
select column-name,aggregate_functions(column-name) from table-name group by column-name;

Having clause:-
---
The having clause is used to filter grouped recprds after group by clause. it is similar to where but it works on grouped instead of individual rows.

--> syntax:-
select column-name,aggreage_function(column-name) from table-name group by column-name having condition;


Order by clause:-
---
The order by clause is used to sort the results in ascending or descending 

-->syntax:-
select column-name from table-name order by column-name;


---> Execution order of clauses:-

QUERY-1
DISPLAY THE AVERAGE MARKS OF EACHDEPARTMENT CONSIDERING ONLY STUDENTS OLDER THAN 20 YEARS.SHOW DEPARTMENTS WHOSE AVERAGE MARKS ARE GREATER THAN 70 AND SORT THE RESULTS BY AVERAGE MARKS IN DESCENDING ORDER.

SELECT DEPARTMENT,AVG(MARKS)AS AVERAGE_MARKS FROM STUDENTS WHERE AGE>20
GROUP BY DEPARTMENT
HAVING AVERAGE_MARKS>70
ORDER BY AVERAGE_MARKS DESC;

QUERY-2
DISPLAY THE TOTAL NUMBER OF STUDENTS IN EACH CITY CONSIDERING ONLY STUDENTS WHOSE MARKS ARE GREATER THAN 70. SHOW CITIES HAVING MORE THAN ONE STUDENT AND SORT BY TOTAL STUDENTS.

SELECT CITY,COUNT(*)AS TOTAL_STUDENTS FROM STUDENTS WHERE MARKS>70
GROUP BY CITY
HAVING TOTAL_STUDENTS>70
ORDER BY TOTAL_STUDENTS;

QUERY-3
DISPLAY THE TOTAL MARKS OF EACH DEPARTMENT CONSIDERING ONLY STUDENTS FROM HYDERABAD AND PUNE.SHOW DEPARTMENTS WHOSE TOTAL MARKS ARE GREATER THAN 100 AND ORDER BY TOTAL MARKS

SELECT DEPARTMENT,SUM(MARKS)AS TOTAL_MARKS FROM STUDENTS WHERE CITY IN('HYDERABAD','PUNE') GROUP BY DEPARTMENT HAVING TOTAL_MARKS>100 ORDER BY TOTAL_MARKS;

QUERY-4
DISPLAY THE HIGHEST MARKS OBTAINED IN EACH CITY CONISDERING ONLY STUDENTS FROM THE ECE DEPARTMENT. SHOW CITIES WHOSE HIGHEST MARKS ARE GREATER THAN 70 AND ORDER BY HIGHEST MARKS

SELECT CITY,MAX(MARKS)AS HIGHEST_MARKS FROM STUDENTS WHERE DEPARTMENT IN('ECE')
GROUP BY CITY
HAVING HIGHEST_MARKS>70
ORDER BY HIGHEST_MARKS;

DISPLAY THE AVERAGE AGE OF STUDENTS IN EACH DEPARTMENT CONSIDERING ONLY STUDENTS WHOSE CITY IS NOT NULL. SHOW DEPARTMENT WHOSE AVERAGE AGE IS GREATER THAN 20 AND SORT ALPHABETICALLY.
