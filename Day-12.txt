1.ENTITY:- TABLES

2.ATTRIBUTE:- COMPOSITE ATTRIBUTE,KEY ATRRIBUTE,MULTIVALUED ATTRIBUTE,DERIVED ATTRIBUTE

3.RELATIONSHIPS:- ONE TO ONE, ONE TO MANY, MANY TO MANY
